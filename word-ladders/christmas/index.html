Every ladder on these sheets starts or ends on a Christmas or winter word — RED, BOX, LOG and HAT on the easy sheet; GIFT, SNOW, SLED, WRAP, SOCK, BELL and CARD at intermediate; STARS, BELLS, CANDY, SLEDS and SCARF at advanced — so the set works as a last-week-of-term activity without changing the puzzle. The words come from the same hand-checked lists as the main sets, and each ladder is verified to be solvable in the number of steps shown.

One easy sheet (3-letter CVC words, kindergarten and 1st grade), four intermediate sheets (4-letter words, 2nd–3rd grade) and two advanced sheets (5-letter words, 4th–6th grade). Four ladders per sheet, answer page behind each, US Letter. The easy sheet has one Christmas word per ladder, because only a handful of CVC words read as Christmas; the Winter set has two more easy sheets.

Frequently asked questions

Which sheet is right for my class?
Easy for children still sounding out three-letter words, intermediate for fluent 7- to 9-year-old readers, advanced for 9 to 12. Every sheet uses different ladders, so a class can do one sheet and keep another for later in the week.
Are the puzzles checked?
Yes. Every word comes from a hand-checked list, and each ladder is verified in code to be solvable in the number of steps shown. The answer page shows one solution; other correct ladders are possible.
Is there an easy Christmas sheet?
Yes, one. Every ladder on it uses three-letter CVC words and starts or ends on RED, BOX, LOG or HAT. For more at the same level, the Winter set has two easy sheets.
Do I have to sign up?
No. Every sheet opens as a PDF straight away.
