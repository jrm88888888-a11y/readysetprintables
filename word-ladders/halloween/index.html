Frequently asked questions

Which sheet is right for my class?
Easy for children still sounding out three-letter words, intermediate for fluent 7- to 9-year-old readers, advanced for 9 to 12. Each pair of sheets uses different ladders, so a class can do one sheet and keep the other for later in the week.
Are the Halloween words scary?
No. The word lists exclude anything a cautious teacher could object to; the Halloween anchors are words like bat, web, mask, cape, bone, black, trick and spell.
Are the puzzles checked?
Yes. Every word comes from a hand-checked list, and each ladder is verified in code to be solvable in the number of steps shown. The answer page shows one solution; other correct ladders are possible.
Do I have to sign up?
No. Every sheet opens as a PDF straight away, and the download-all button gives you all six in one file.
