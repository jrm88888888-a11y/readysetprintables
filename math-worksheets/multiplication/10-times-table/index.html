10 Times Table Worksheets

Free printable 10 times table worksheets as PDFs. Ordered, mixed and missing-number sheets for 10 x 1 to 10 x 12. No sign-up.

The ten times table is the one children learn without noticing, and it is also the one most often taught with a phrase that causes trouble later: add a zero. It works for whole numbers and then fails the moment decimals appear, because 10 x 2.5 is not 2.50. The honest version is that multiplying by ten shifts every digit one place to the left. These sheets keep the practice quick, since the facts themselves rarely need drilling, and use the space instead for missing numbers and division.

Ordered sheets from 10 x 1 to 10 x 12, mixed-order recall, and a larger proportion of missing-number and division rows than the other tables, since the forward facts are usually secure early. Some sheets extend past 12 into larger multiples to reinforce the place-value idea. One worksheet per US Letter page, black and white.

Frequently asked questions

Should I teach add a zero for multiplying by 10?
It is a useful shortcut for whole numbers but it breaks with decimals, and children who learn it as a rule often apply it to 2.5 and get 2.50. The more durable explanation is that every digit moves one place to the left, which stays true for decimals and for dividing by ten in reverse.
Why is the 10 times table so important?
Because our number system is built on tens, so it underpins place value, mental strategies for other tables, and later work on decimals and percentages. Knowing 10 x 7 also gives you a route to 5 x 7 by halving and to 9 x 7 by subtracting 7.
How can the 10 times table help with harder tables?
By subtracting. 9 x 6 is 10 x 6 minus 6, and 11 x 6 is 10 x 6 plus 6. Anchoring on ten and adjusting is often faster than recalling a shaky fact directly, and it works for larger numbers too.
What age do children learn it?
As an editorial guide, usually around ages 5 to 7, often before formal multiplication is introduced, through counting in tens. Most children can count in tens some time before they can answer 10 x 7 out of order, which is what the mixed sheets here are for.
