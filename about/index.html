About Ready Set Printables

Ready Set Printables exists to solve one small, real annoyance: when you search for something like “dinosaur coloring pages” you often land on a maze of links, sign-up walls and pop-ups before you can print a single sheet. Here, the printable is the page. Click, download the PDF, print. That’s it.

Free to use

Everything is free to download and print for your home or classroom. There is no charge, no account and no email required. You’re welcome to print as many copies as you need. Please don’t re-sell the files or re-host them as your own.

How it’s made

Logic puzzles (Calcudoku, nonograms, sudoku, hashi and more) are generated and checked in code so each one has a single valid solution. Word ladders use a hand-checked list of real, age-appropriate words and are verified to be solvable in the number of steps shown; the answer page shows one solution and other correct ladders are possible. Worksheets are laid out for clear, single-page printing. If you spot a mistake on any sheet, we’d love to hear about it.

A sister site

We also make AI Study Method: 1,960 free interactive mini-lessons, quizzes and games for middle and high school (grades 7–12) in science, math, history, geography, psychology, computer science and languages. It is free in the same way this site is, with no login and no ads, and teachers can assign any lesson with a link or QR code. If you know a middle- or high-school teacher, it is the thing to pass on.
