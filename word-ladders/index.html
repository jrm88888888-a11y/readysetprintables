Free Printable Word Ladders

60 free printable word ladder puzzles as PDFs, in six sets: easy CVC ladders for K–1, intermediate for grades 2–3 (with and without clues), advanced for grades 4–6, plus Halloween and Christmas sets. Answer pages included. No sign-up.

A word ladder starts with one word and ends with another; the child changes one letter at a time, and every rung has to be a real word. These sets are built so the puzzle is always fair: every word comes from a hand-checked list of real, age-appropriate words, and each ladder is verified to be solvable in the number of steps shown. The answer page shows one solution, and any ladder where each step changes one letter and makes a real word is also correct.

Six sets: Easy (3-letter CVC words, 4 steps, kindergarten and 1st grade), Intermediate (4-letter words, 5 steps, 2nd and 3rd grade), Intermediate with Clues (a short clue beside every hidden rung), Advanced (5-letter words, 6 steps, 4th to 6th grade), and Halloween and Christmas sets at every level. Each sheet is one US Letter page of four ladders with the answer page behind it, and every set has a download-all PDF.
