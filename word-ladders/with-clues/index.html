A clued word ladder gives the child a definition for every hidden rung — 'a farm building for animals and hay' — so the task shifts from open search to vocabulary: read the clue, work out the word, check that it is one letter away from the rung above. These sheets suit children who find blank ladders frustrating, and they work well for independent or homework use because the clue makes each step self-checking.

10 sheets of three ladders each, on US Letter. Every hidden rung has a clue of nine words or fewer, written in grade 2–3 vocabulary and checked so that no clue contains its answer. The answer page highlights the changed letter at each step.

Frequently asked questions

How is this different from the intermediate set?
Same word list and the same five-step, four-letter ladders. The difference is the clue printed beside every hidden rung. Use this set first if a child is new to word ladders, then move to the blank intermediate sheets.
Are the clues checked?
Yes. Each clue is checked automatically so that it never contains its answer word and stays under ten words, and the set was reviewed for clues that could fit a rival word one letter away.
What age are these for?
2nd and 3rd grade (ages 7–9). Younger children can do them with an adult reading the clues aloud.
Do I have to sign up?
No. Every sheet opens as a PDF straight away.
