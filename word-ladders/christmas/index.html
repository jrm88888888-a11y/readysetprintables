Every ladder on these sheets starts or ends on a Christmas or winter word — BELL, GIFT, CARD, SOCK, COLD and CAKE on the intermediate sheets; STARS, BELLS, CANDY, SLEDS, CAKES and SCARF at advanced — so the set works as a last-week-of-term activity without changing the puzzle. The words come from the same hand-checked lists as the main sets, and each ladder is verified to be solvable in the number of steps shown.

Two intermediate sheets (4-letter words, 2nd–3rd grade) and two advanced sheets (5-letter words, 4th–6th grade). Four ladders per sheet, answer page behind each, US Letter. There is no easy Christmas sheet because too few Christmas words are three-letter CVC words; the easy set and the Halloween easy sheets suit kindergarten and 1st grade.

Frequently asked questions

Which sheet is right for my class?
Intermediate for fluent 7- to 9-year-old readers, advanced for 9 to 12. The two sheets at each level use different ladders.
Are the puzzles checked?
Yes. Every word comes from a hand-checked list, and each ladder is verified in code to be solvable in the number of steps shown. The answer page shows one solution; other correct ladders are possible.
Is there an easy Christmas sheet?
Not yet. Three-letter CVC words with a Christmas link are too few to build fair ladders from. The easy set on this site uses everyday CVC words and suits the same age.
Do I have to sign up?
No. Every sheet opens as a PDF straight away.
